Visual Timer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Visual Timer ("the app", on the App Store as Visual Timer: Kids Timer) is a countdown timer for children, made by Brian Kershner.

What is collected

One thing: purchase history, when you buy Visual Timer Pro. The purchase is processed by Apple, and we use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, to confirm it. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous identifier and your purchase history for this app. It is not linked to your name, your Apple ID email or anything else about you. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

That is what the App Store privacy label says: Purchases, not linked to you. Nothing else is collected.

What stays on your iPhone

Your settings, saved timers, routines and history are kept only in the app's storage on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone. There is no account and no server, and the app works offline. Delete the app and they go with it.

Timer alerts are local notifications, scheduled on the iPhone itself. If you start a free trial and allow notifications, the app also schedules one local reminder the day before the trial ends.

Children

The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, asks for no personal information, and nothing on the timer screen leads out of the app.
