- 2026-11-17: 1.0.0 build 1, step 4 of 7: screens, saved timers, routines, history, settings.
  - Timer (src/screens/TimerHome.tsx): a dial (one turn is 60 minutes, stops at 1 and 60, a tick each minute, VoiceOver adjustable) with the chosen scene in its middle, the time in words, the presets 1, 2, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45 and 60 minutes as big chips, the scene picker (live thumbnails, a lock on Pro ones) and, for the disc, the color picker.   - Running (src/screens/Run.tsx): the scene as big as the screen allows, the digits under it (a setting), pause, +1, +5, restart, and Next for a routine. Stop asks first. Portrait and landscape layouts. Classroom mode (Pro) locks to landscape with the scene and huge digits side by side. Child lock (Pro, or on by itself from Settings): the controls go away, every touch shows "Locked. A grown-up can hold a corner for 2 seconds.", and holding any corner for 2 seconds (a ring fills) unlocks. Done: the scene's ending, "All done", Again, 1 minute, Close. Routines show the steps as a row of icons (ticked, current ringed), "Next: ..." and a 5 second "Well done" with five dots going out before the next step.
  - src/runner.tsx: owns the run. Stored on every change; a run left from before the app was closed is brought up to now and reopened (dropped after 10 minutes if it had finished). Logic ticks 4 times a second (endings, the one-minute warning, ticking), the scene redraws about 24 times a second (4 with Reduce Motion). Keeps the screen awake while the run screen is up. Schedules the end alert, or one per routine step, after every change, and cancels them on pause and stop. The end sound plays only if the end was in the last 4 seconds; otherwise the notification already played it. History gets one entry per finished timer or routine.
  - Saved timers (src/screens/Saved.tsx): one tap starts; the pencil opens the editor (name, picture, minutes, scene, disc color, delete). Free users run and change the first one; the rest and New timer show PRO.
  - Routines (src/screens/Routines.tsx): Morning, Bedtime, Leaving the house to start; editor with steps in order (up, down, change, remove, add), one scene for every step, a picture. Pro.
  - History (src/screens/History.tsx): by day, "Today", "Yesterday", then dates, with a count and minutes per day.
  - Settings (src/screens/Settings.tsx), only through the 2 second hold on the gear: end sound (tap to hear), one minute left, ticking, buzz, digits, child lock on start, Pro, restore, email support, help page, privacy policy, clear history, version. - 2026-11-17: 1.0.0 build 1, step 3 of 7: nine scenes, drawn in code with react-native-svg.
  - Why SVG and not Skia: either was allowed. react-native-svg 15.15.4 is already proven on RN 0.86 in Signature Maker, it has no CanvasKit or load-order risk, and the same components render on react-native-web, so the design renders and store screenshots come from the real scene code. Each scene redraws about 24 times a second while a timer runs (see the runner in step 4); the drawings are a few dozen shapes each.
  - Every scene is a function of two numbers: f, the time left (1 to 0), and t, a clock for small living touches. Free: Color disc (default teal; the whole disc is always the whole timer, the empty part opens clockwise from twelve, no numbers or minute marks), Sand jar (a glass jar emptying through a spout into a wooden bowl), Caterpillar (eats a leaf from the tip to the stem in round bites, becomes a butterfly at the end).   - Pure shapes in src/scenes/geometry.ts with checks: the wedge path at 1, 0.75, 0.5, 0.25 and 0, the leaf shrinking to the bite edge with bites, the stars count, the rocket's trip and heading, color mixing. A test also checks the scenes use no images and no text, and that no disc color is a red.

- 2026-11-17: 1.0.0 build 1, step 2 of 7: timer engine, routines, sounds, alerts.
  - src/timer.ts (pure): a timer is ready, running, paused or done. Time left is always endAt minus the wall clock, never counted ticks, so it is right after the background, a locked phone, or the app being closed (the state is plain JSON). settle() marks a timer done at its real end time, not when the app looked. Add time moves the end out and grows the whole by the same amount, so the scene grows back by that much; after the end it starts a new run of just the added time. Restart goes back to the length picked. The one-minute warning fires once at a minute left, only on timers over two minutes, and again if time is added past a minute.
  - src/routine.ts (pure): steps in order with a 5 second cheer between them. settleRoutine() walks through every step and cheer that ended while the app was away, using each end time, so a routine left running with the phone locked is in the right step when the app comes back. upcomingStepEnds() gives the notification times, which match the settled ends exactly (tested). Pause, add time, restart and Next work on the current step.
  - src/gates.ts (pure): free is the disc, sand jar and caterpillar, teal, presets and dial, one saved timer, all sounds, history; Pro is everything else. A Pro look on a free account falls back to the teal disc instead of blocking a child on a paywall.
  - store/sounds.js: the five end sounds (soft chime, little bells, marimba, harp, wood drum), a quiet tick and the one-minute warning, synthesized from sine partials, a Karplus-Strong string and filtered noise, so they belong to this app. Peaks are kept low and every note fades in. Not listened to from here: the sandbox has no audio out. Brian should judge them on the phone.
  - src/sound.ts (expo-audio, mixWithOthers, plays with the ring switch on silent) and src/alerts.ts (local notifications with the chosen sound for the end of a timer and every routine step; hidden while the app is open since the app plays the sound itself).
  - Checked: npx tsc --noEmit clean; npm test 24/24 (11 new for timer and routines, 2 for gates and sound files).
