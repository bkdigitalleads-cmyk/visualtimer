# Visual Timer change log

Newest first. One entry per commit: date, version/build, what changed and why, and how it was checked.

- 2026-11-17: 1.0.0 build 1, step 3 of 7: nine scenes, drawn in code with react-native-svg.
  - Why SVG and not Skia: either was allowed. react-native-svg 15.15.4 is already proven on RN 0.86 in Signature Maker, it has no CanvasKit or load-order risk, and the same components render on react-native-web, so the design renders and store screenshots come from the real scene code. Each scene redraws about 24 times a second while a timer runs (see the runner in step 4); the drawings are a few dozen shapes each.
  - Every scene is a function of two numbers: f, the time left (1 to 0), and t, a clock for small living touches. Free: Color disc (default teal; the whole disc is always the whole timer, the empty part opens clockwise from twelve, no numbers or minute marks), Sand jar (a glass jar emptying through a spout into a wooden bowl), Caterpillar (eats a leaf from the tip to the stem in round bites, becomes a butterfly at the end). Pro: Candle (burns down, smoke curl at the end), Rocket (dotted path to the moon is the time left, lands and plants a flag), Ice pop (three colors melt from the top, the stick shows, the puddle grows), Train (the track to the station is the time left), Stars (ten stars go out from the last; the one going out drains from the top), Sunset (the sun goes down behind the hills, the sky turns to night, a window lights up).
  - Each has its ending (sparkles, the butterfly, the flag, the moon) and a dark version that follows the system theme.
  - Pure shapes in src/scenes/geometry.ts with checks: the wedge path at 1, 0.75, 0.5, 0.25 and 0, the leaf shrinking to the bite edge with bites, the stars count, the rocket's trip and heading, color mixing. A test also checks the scenes use no images and no text, and that no disc color is a red.
  - Looked at in a throwaway react-native-web harness (scratchpad, not in the repo): every scene at 1, 0.62, 0.25, 0.04 and done, light and dark. Fixed from that look: the train now travels 136 points instead of 114, the sun sets in a straight line so it is half behind the hills at a quarter left, and the ice pop's drips stop on the last sliver.
  - Checked: npx tsc --noEmit clean; npm test 30/30.
- 2026-11-17: 1.0.0 build 1, step 2 of 7: timer engine, routines, sounds, alerts.
  - src/timer.ts (pure): a timer is ready, running, paused or done. Time left is always endAt minus the wall clock, never counted ticks, so it is right after the background, a locked phone, or the app being closed (the state is plain JSON). settle() marks a timer done at its real end time, not when the app looked. Add time moves the end out and grows the whole by the same amount, so the scene grows back by that much; after the end it starts a new run of just the added time. Restart goes back to the length picked. The one-minute warning fires once at a minute left, only on timers over two minutes, and again if time is added past a minute.
  - src/routine.ts (pure): steps in order with a 5 second cheer between them. settleRoutine() walks through every step and cheer that ended while the app was away, using each end time, so a routine left running with the phone locked is in the right step when the app comes back. upcomingStepEnds() gives the notification times, which match the settled ends exactly (tested). Pause, add time, restart and Next work on the current step.
  - src/gates.ts (pure): free is the disc, sand jar and caterpillar, teal, presets and dial, one saved timer, all sounds, history; Pro is everything else. A Pro look on a free account falls back to the teal disc instead of blocking a child on a paywall.
  - store/sounds.js: the five end sounds (soft chime, little bells, marimba, harp, wood drum), a quiet tick and the one-minute warning, synthesized from sine partials, a Karplus-Strong string and filtered noise, so they belong to this app. Peaks are kept low and every note fades in. Not listened to from here: the sandbox has no audio out. Brian should judge them on the phone.
  - src/sound.ts (expo-audio, mixWithOthers, plays with the ring switch on silent) and src/alerts.ts (local notifications with the chosen sound for the end of a timer and every routine step; hidden while the app is open since the app plays the sound itself).
  - Checked: npx tsc --noEmit clean; npm test 24/24 (11 new for timer and routines, 2 for gates and sound files).
