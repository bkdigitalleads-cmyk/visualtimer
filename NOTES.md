- 2026-11-17: 1.0.0 build 1, step 2 of 7: timer engine, routines, sounds, alerts.
  - src/timer.ts (pure): a timer is ready, running, paused or done. Time left is always endAt minus the wall clock, never counted ticks, so it is right after the background, a locked phone, or the app being closed (the state is plain JSON). settle() marks a timer done at its real end time, not when the app looked. Add time moves the end out and grows the whole by the same amount, so the scene grows back by that much; after the end it starts a new run of just the added time. Restart goes back to the length picked. The one-minute warning fires once at a minute left, only on timers over two minutes, and again if time is added past a minute.
  - src/routine.ts (pure): steps in order with a 5 second cheer between them. settleRoutine() walks through every step and cheer that ended while the app was away, using each end time, so a routine left running with the phone locked is in the right step when the app comes back. upcomingStepEnds() gives the notification times, which match the settled ends exactly (tested). Pause, add time, restart and Next work on the current step.
  - src/gates.ts (pure): free is the disc, sand jar and caterpillar, teal, presets and dial, one saved timer, all sounds, history; Pro is everything else. A Pro look on a free account falls back to the teal disc instead of blocking a child on a paywall.
  - store/sounds.js: the five end sounds (soft chime, little bells, marimba, harp, wood drum), a quiet tick and the one-minute warning, synthesized from sine partials, a Karplus-Strong string and filtered noise, so they belong to this app. Peaks are kept low and every note fades in. Not listened to from here: the sandbox has no audio out. Brian should judge them on the phone.
  - src/sound.ts (expo-audio, mixWithOthers, plays with the ring switch on silent) and src/alerts.ts (local notifications with the chosen sound for the end of a timer and every routine step; hidden while the app is open since the app plays the sound itself).
  - Checked: npx tsc --noEmit clean; npm test 24/24 (11 new for timer and routines, 2 for gates and sound files).
